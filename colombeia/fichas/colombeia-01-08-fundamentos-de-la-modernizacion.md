Aborda a Guzmán Blanco como un positivista práctico con sus propuestas para reformar la educación y la infraestructura desde la óptica comtiana.'

This paper analyzes his political thought regarding the notion of "Order and Progress," evaluating how he used these ideas to diagnose the country''s problems and propose concrete solutions. It approaches Guzmán Blanco as a practical positivist through his proposals to reform education and infrastructure from a Comtian perspective.'
